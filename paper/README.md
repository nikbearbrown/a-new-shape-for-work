# A New Shape for Work

[Read the rough first draft](manuscript.md). It presents the six-phase model, reports the prototype's first results, and sets out how to test whether those results reflect work or how we measure it.

The manuscript is open for review. The most useful next contributions are a check of one claim or citation, an occupation-specific counterexample, or independent worker-side evidence. [How to contribute](../CONTRIBUTING.md) lists specific tasks. [Research data](../research-data/) has the reproducible tables and source manifest; [Methods](../docs/METHOD.md) documents what the measures can and cannot support.
